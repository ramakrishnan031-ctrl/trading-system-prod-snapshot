B1. DOES 15:15 / 15:17 FORCE-CLOSE A CNC POSITION?  *** NO — REGISTER REFUTED. ***
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[SOURCE, three independent sites]
 · The 15:15 "circuit breaker" is a SOFT_KILL, reason
   `circuit_breaker_force_close_15:15` (`capital/kill_switch.py:115`, and `:331`
   names it a normal daily scheduled kill). SOFT_KILL blocks ENTRIES and allows
   exits; `is_blocked(intent="exit")` is True only for HARD_KILL (`:476-484`).
   *** It closes nothing. ***
 · The 15:17 EOD squareoff EXPLICITLY excludes delivery. EOD6, stated in the
   module docstring: "DELIVERY (CNC) positions NOT touched. Only INTRADAY (MIS)
   and COVER_ORDER (CO) products exited" (`orders/eod_squareoff.py:23-24`,`:34`).
   Enforced by filter `p.product in ("MIS","CO")` (`:1064-1072`, FIX-015).
 · The FIX-182 residual broker flatten in the SAME file also filters
   `product in ("MIS","CO")` (`:1439`) and its docstring says non-system CNC
   positions "are intentionally NOT squared off" (`:1411-1419`).
⇒ *** The register's claim that "CNC positions would be force-squared without the
Slice 2.5 lifecycle fix" is FALSE. The product filter is already there, at both
sites. *** This is the second premise in two days to be refuted from source.

⚠️⚠️ BUT THERE IS A REAL FINDING NEXT DOOR, AND IT IS THE OPPOSITE SHAPE:
*** HARD_KILL DOES FLATTEN CNC — DELIBERATELY — AND FROM T+1 IT CANNOT. ***
 · The flatten's trade SELECT has NO product filter: it selects every trade in
   ('OPEN','PARTIAL','PENDING_FILL') and fetches `product` only to exit under the
   SAME product ("MIS vs CNC matters", `kill_switch.py:1463-1479`, `:1510`).
 · The FIX-181 LAYER A broker sweep (`:1567-1611`) reads `get_positions()` and
   flattens ANY non-zero position not already handled — filtering only on symbol,
   qty and already-handled. It maps CNC->DELIVERY intent on purpose (H-5,
   `:1582-1590`) so the CNC exit actually offsets.
 · Its stated invariant: "A HARD_KILL must leave NO live broker position".
⇒ ON THE BUY DAY (T) a delivery position IS in positions() ⇒ a HARD_KILL WILL
  sell it. [INFERENCE from the T-day/T+1 mechanic + Wednesday's ORPHAN row proving
  the position was visible in positions() on T.] That is arguably CORRECT for an
  emergency, but it must be a KNOWN, DELIBERATE consequence of the flip.
⇒ FROM T+1 the holding is in holdings() only [MEASURED today: get_positions()
  returns 0 while holdings() returns all five] ⇒ *** the sweep CANNOT see it, and
  HARD_KILL's "no live broker position" invariant is FALSE for delivery. *** A
  HARD_KILL would report success while leaving real delivery inventory exposed.
⭐ NOTE FOR THE T2 TEST, WHICH IS LIVE RIGHT NOW: the basket was exposed to a

B2. DOES ANYTHING COMPARE THIS MORNING'S HOLDINGS TO LAST NIGHT'S INVENTORY?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
*** NO. *** [SOURCE] Nothing persists a closing inventory, so nothing can compare
to it. `position_reconciliation` stores a same-day snapshot only, and only of
`positions()`. FM9 at 09:15 compares CASH. `CncGttMonitor` compares GTT coverage
against current holdings, not against yesterday's expectation.
⇒ Rama's point 3 is a GENUINE GAP. The 09:15 hook exists; the stored expectation
does not.

B3. IS DAILY P&L / CAPITAL CORRECT WITH UNREALIZED OVERNIGHT POSITIONS?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PARTLY, AND ONE PART IS OPEN. [SOURCE + prior MEASURED]
 · The daily-loss limit reads `fm_ledger.pnl_delta`, not `trades.net_pnl`. An
   OPEN delivery position writes no pnl_delta until it closes ⇒ *** the daily-loss
   limit is blind to unrealized overnight drawdown, and the whole loss lands in
   the P&L of the day the position is finally SOLD. ***
 · The FM baseline self-heals: the 08:15 boot re-seeds from `broker.net`, which is
   already net of cash blocked by the holding ⇒ no drift from the carry itself
   [MEASURED precedent 29->30 Jul].
⇒ GENUINE GAP, but *** possibly a deliberate scope choice *** (daily loss =
realized intraday risk). ⛔ I am NOT calling this a defect. OPEN QUESTION Q3.

B4. WHAT HAPPENS TO A REAL DELIVERY POSITION'S GTT AT BOOT?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IT IS ADOPTED, NOT WARNED — the warnings are the artefact. [SOURCE]
`adopt_orphan_gtts()` matches a broker GTT to a candidate local CNC trade; with a
candidate it INSERTs a `gtt_state` row (adoption); with none it emits
`adopt_no_trade:<SYMBOL>` and a WARNING. T2 has no local trade (isolated DB) ⇒
candidates == 0 ⇒ the five warnings. A real delivery trade lives in the live DB ⇒
it has a candidate ⇒ adopted silently. Adoption only ever INSERTs or WARNs, never
deletes; `_orphan_sweep_and_cap` never touches a GTT with no gtt_state row
(FIX-182).
⇒ B4 is a TEST ARTEFACT. No design work needed. ⚠️ Its corollary is a real
one though: once adopted, `delivery_symbols` becomes non-empty and starts
excluding those symbols from the orphan sweep (`:847`,`:904`) — a path that has
NEVER run in production, because `delivery_symbols` has always been empty.

B5. DO THE 15:45 EXIT CODES / ALERTS BEHAVE ON A LEGITIMATE DELIVERY HOLDING?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
*** NO — AND IT IS WORSE THAN THE CARD GUESSED. *** [SOURCE, high confidence]
The card expected a daily false ORPHAN_AT_BROKER. The actual failure is the
MIRROR of that, and it fires from T+1 for the whole holding period:
    broker_positions from positions()  = {} (holding is invisible from T+1)
    system_positions from trades       = {SYM: qty} (still OPEN — correctly so)
    ⇒ broker_qty == 0 and system_qty != 0  ⇒ *** MISSING_AT_BROKER *** (`:267-269`)
    ⇒ log.critical + ERROR Telegram + exit 2, EVERY DAY THE POSITION IS HELD.
*** "MISSING_AT_BROKER" is the single most alarming string this system can emit
about a position — it reads as "our stock is gone" — and it would fire daily on a
perfectly healthy delivery holding. *** That is how alert fatigue starts, on the
capital path, on day one of delivery.
⇒ GENUINE GAP. Highest-priority item in this document.

B6. DOES A LEGITIMATE CNC HOLDING GRANT Rs 5,000 OF BLINDNESS DAILY?

*** NO — TEST ARTEFACT. *** [SOURCE] CHECK2 classifies HUMAN_ORDER only when

⇒ *** The FIX-182 concern carried into the asking card does NOT belong in this

D. PROPOSED DESIGN
================================================================================
OBJECTIVE: make EOD and next-morning reconciliation correct for a book that can
hold stock overnight, WITHOUT adding a second reconciler.

D-1. THE SHAPE: ADD ONE `delivery` DIMENSION TO `eod_broker_reconcile` (15:58),
     FED BY THE EXISTING `CncGttMonitor._gather()` HELD-QTY READER.
     · Broker side: holdings() + CNC positions(), merged — the existing reader,
       lifted to a shared helper. It is the ONLY read that is correct across the
       T/T+1 boundary. ⛔ Do not write a third definition of "held".
     · System side: expected closing inventory per symbol =
       opening + buys − sells, derived from `trades`/`orders`, NOT stored twice.
     · Verdict: reuse VERIFIED / ISSUES / UNVERIFIED. Broker holdings
       unavailable ⇒ UNVERIFIED, never a false VERIFIED (the existing rule).
     · Classification: REQUIRED once `delivery_enabled` is true; NOT_CHECKED while
       it is false — so the dimension can ship BEFORE the flip, inert, and be
       exercised by the flip rather than by a redesign.

D-2. PERSIST A CLOSING INVENTORY SNAPSHOT (new, small) so a morning comparison is
     possible at all. One row per symbol per trading date, written by the 15:58
     job. *** Friday's snapshot is simply the most recent one *** — "Friday EOD
     becomes Monday's opening" needs NO weekend special-case if the morning check
     reads "the last trading day's snapshot" rather than "yesterday". ⛔ Do not
     build a calendar rule where a MAX(date) does the job.

D-3. MOVE THE 09:15 CHECK ONTO THE EXISTING FM9 ONE-SHOT (do not add a cron):
     compare broker holdings at 09:15 against the last snapshot; mismatch ⇒ ONE
     consolidated alert. This is the only place a corporate action, an unexpected
     settlement, or an overnight broker-side change becomes visible.

D-4. FIX G1 AT ITS ROOT, NOT WITH A SUPPRESSION: `reconcile_positions` must stop
     comparing a delivery trade against `positions()`. Two candidates, and I
     recommend the first:
     (a) *** Scope it honestly to intraday: *** exclude trades whose product is a
         delivery product from `_get_system_positions`, and rename what it reports
         to MIS. It then does one thing correctly and the delivery dimension in
         (D-1) owns delivery. ⭐ Smallest change, removes the false CRITICAL,
         removes the green-check-that-cannot-go-red.
     (b) Teach it holdings too — rejected: it duplicates D-1 and leaves two jobs
         alerting on the same axis 13 minutes apart.
